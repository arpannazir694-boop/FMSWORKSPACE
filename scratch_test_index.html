ABOUT TRIO GROUP

Legacy of Craftsmanship Since 1978

Established in 1978 by the visionary Mr. Mukund Vasudeo Kulkarni, Trio Group has grown into an international benchmark for luxury handbag manufacturing and leather engineering.

Headquartered with world-class facilities in Kolkata, Trio Group collaborates with leading fashion houses across Paris, London, Milan, New York, and Amsterdam. Every collection represents a seamless dialogue between timeless handcrafting artistry and progressive engineering.

The FMS Workspace represents our commitment to operational transparency—a centralized digital nervous system that tracks every hide from tannery procurement and automated die-less cutting to two-tier quality control and international air-cargo dispatch.
